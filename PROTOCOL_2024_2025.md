# Two-year rerun protocol (declared 2026-10-15, before fetching 2025 data)

Nothing below is changed after 2025 results are seen; any deviation is
reported as a deviation.

## Data

- Window: 2024-01-02 to 2025-12-31 UTC, the same seven regions.
- 2025 fuel mix and load forecasts: `scripts/fetch_unified.py --year 2025`,
  same datasets and `latest_before` publication rule as 2024.
- ERCOT wind and solar forecasts for 2024 and 2025: forecast columns only
  (STWPF/WGRPP/COP HSL, STPPF/PVGRPP/COP HSL), latest publication no later
  than the target hour.
- Before any model is fitted, report unique target hours and interpolated
  gaps per region and year (IESO in particular, after its May 2025 market
  change). No region is dropped on the basis of results.

## Held fixed

Configs, features, hyperparameters, loss weights, emission factors, the 1/6
scheduling cap, main seeds 7/42/123, the ten ERCOT audit seeds, and the SPO+
weight lambda=10 (not reselected).

## Experiments

- Fixed split: chronological 70/15/15 over the two-year table.
- Rolling origins: 2025 test windows starting Feb 1, Apr 1, Jun 1, Aug 1,
  and Oct 1; 56 test days, 30 validation days, expanding history from the
  start of the data. The 2024 windows (Jun/Aug/Oct) are kept as the
  short-history contrast; Jun/Aug/Oct 2024 and 2025 are compared pairwise.
- Intervals: the existing independent-calibration and OOF protocol (seed 42,
  80%) on the two-year table.
- ERCOT MSE/SPO+ 2x2 and the ten-seed audit on the 2025 rolling windows.
- Fuel-contribution check on the new fixed-split test period.
- ERCOT renewable variant: add the wind/solar forecast features to both Direct
  MLP and Fuel-mix, and rerun the fixed split, rolling windows, and
  contribution check. It is reported beside the ERCOT baseline and does not
  replace the main results.

## Expectations stated in advance

1. If short training history drove the 2024 rolling deficit, the June 2025
   Fuel-mix/persistence RMSE ratio falls clearly below its June 2024 range
   (1.06-3.28) toward the later-window levels.
2. In ERCOT, Direct SPO+ remains at least as good as both MSE ensembles.
3. Without new features, fuel-contribution accuracy stays close to 24-hour
   fuel persistence.
4. With ERCOT wind/solar forecasts, the gas-contribution error falls: in the
   November 2024 case window, gas generation dropped from about 26 GW to
   5-8 GW while wind rose from under 5 GW to 20-25 GW.

## Addendum (2026-10-15, after the data check, before any model fit)

Data check of the fetched files:

- All 2025 fuel-mix and load files cover 8,760 target hours, with columns
  identical to 2024, except the IESO load forecast: 8,651 hours, one
  contiguous gap of 109 hours from 2025-05-02 06:00 UTC, right after the IESO
  market renewal. As in 2024, the gap hours only are filled from the
  post-renewal zonal feed (`ieso_zonal_load_forecast_hourly`, Ontario total,
  latest publication no later than the target hour), and every row keeps a
  `forecast_source` label.
- ERCOT wind/solar forecasts selected by "latest publication no later than
  the target hour" have a median lead of 0.08 h, against 15.5 h for the ERCOT
  load forecast. That declared variant is kept and reported as an upper bound
  on near-real-time information. A second variant uses the latest
  publication at least 24 hours before the target hour, consistent with the
  day-ahead framing; it is fitted and reported in the same way.
- ERCOT `power_storage` (mapped to `other`) becomes a signed net series from
  2025-12-05, the RTC+B go-live; 409 charging hours are negative. Negative
  values are clipped to zero, as already done for CAISO, MISO, and IESO; the
  2024 series has no negative values, so 2024 inputs are unchanged.
- The November 2024 ERCOT case window lies outside the new test period. The
  case window is the contiguous 72-hour window with the largest realized-proxy
  range in the new ERCOT fixed-split test period, chosen from realized data
  only and shown for the baseline and both wind/solar variants.
- All fits run single-threaded (`OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`) so that retraining in the
  contribution check reproduces the point-forecast table exactly.
- Deviation: the IESO zonal fallback has no forecast published before its
  target hour until 2025-05-08 17:00 UTC, so it cannot fill the gap without
  look-ahead and is not used. The 109 gap hours, and the 24 following hours
  whose lagged inputs fall in the gap, are dropped (133 IESO hours; one
  timestamp jump from 2025-05-02 05:00 to 2025-05-07 19:00). Interpolation is
  limited to gaps of at most 6 hours (only PJM has such gaps); the 2024 tables
  are identical under the old and new code for all seven regions in both
  settings. Scheduling evaluation keeps only complete UTC days.
